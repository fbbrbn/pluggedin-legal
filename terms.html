1. What PluggedIn is

PluggedIn is an app that plans when to charge your electric car, based on Danish electricity spot prices and your grid tariffs, and — if you connect a compatible charger — starts and stops that charge for you.

It is provided by Fabio Barboni, Denmark, contact pluggedin.app.support@gmail.com.

3. Prices and savings figures are estimates

Prices shown in the app come from public sources (Energi Data Service and your configured tariffs). Savings figures are calculations based on those numbers.

They are estimates, not a bill. Real cost can differ because of tariff changes, supplier fees, metering differences, taxes, rounding, or simply because a source published something wrong. Your supplier's invoice is the authoritative figure — not PluggedIn.

You are responsible for keeping your tariff settings accurate. The app cannot know what you actually pay unless you tell it.

4. Charging may not happen when planned

Automatic charging depends on things outside our control: your internet connection, your phone's battery and power-saving behaviour, Android's scheduling decisions, your charger manufacturer's cloud service, your car, and your home electrical supply.

Any of these can cause a scheduled charge to start late, stop early, or not happen at all. If it genuinely matters that your car is charged by a particular time — a long trip, a medical need, work — do not rely solely on PluggedIn. Check the car, or charge it directly.

We do not guarantee availability, uptime, or that any particular charge will occur.
